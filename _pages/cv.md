Research and Work Experience
======
* **September 2024–Present: Engineering Excellence Postdoctoral Fellow**, Institute for Medical Engineering and Science, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, MA, USA
  * Advisor: James J. Collins, Department of Biological Engineering
  * Engineering microbial living therapeutics
  * Targeted microbial consortia and microbiome engineering
  * AI-guided antimicrobial systems development

* **2018–2024: Ph.D. Student and Interim Postdoctoral Scholar**, Center for Synthetic Biology, Molecular Engineering and Sciences Institute, University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA
  * Advisors: Jesse G. Zalatan, Department of Chemistry, and James M. Carothers, Department of Chemical Engineering; collaborated with interdisciplinary teams at UW, PNNL, and LBNL
  * Developed bacterial CRISPR transcriptional-modulation tools
  * Engineered carbon-conserving metabolism in diverse bacteria, including *Pseudomonas putida*, *Bacillus subtilis*, and *Acinetobacter baylyi*
  * Accelerated design-build-test-learn cycles using tunable CRISPR programs
  * Investigated gene-regulatory circuits at genome scale using CRISPRa/i

* **2017–2018: Research Assistant**, School of Biomolecular Science and Engineering, Vidhyasirimethi Institute of Science and Technology (VISTEC), Thailand
  * Advisor: Pimchai Chaiyen
  * Engineered metabolic pathways in *E. coli* for biofuel synthesis
  * Investigated new chemistry of carboxylic acid reductase enzymes

* **2016–2017: Master's Researcher**, Department of Chemistry, Faculty of Science, Mahidol University, Thailand
  * Advisor: Torsak Luanphaisarnnont; co-advisor: Chutima Jiarpinitnun
  * Synthesized allenic esters and investigated conjugate-addition mechanisms
  * Evaluated antibacterial activity against *Staphylococcus aureus*
  * Studied intramolecular dearomatization Diels–Alder reactions
