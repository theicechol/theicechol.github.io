When I introduce myself as Ice, people often ask the same question: “Is that your real name?”

The short answer is yes—but the longer answer begins with a Thai cultural tradition.

## Why Thai people have nicknames

In Thailand, most people have a nickname, or *ชื่อเล่น* (*chue len*), that family, friends, classmates, and colleagues use in everyday life. These names are often much shorter than our formal given names. They can come from Thai or English words, physical traits, foods, animals, popular culture, family preferences, or simply a word that a parent liked.

A nickname is not merely an occasional term of affection. For many Thai people, it is the name used in nearly every ordinary social setting. Our formal names remain important for official documents and formal occasions, but even people who have known one another for years may primarily know each other by their nicknames.

This can be surprising outside Thailand—especially when the nickname is an English word such as Ice.
