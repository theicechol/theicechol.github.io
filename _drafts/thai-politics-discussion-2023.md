In 2023, I co-hosted discussions with the Association for Thai Democracy, USA, exploring Thai politics from the perspective of people living both inside and outside the country. The conversations connected questions about everyday opportunities with the institutions shaping Thailand’s political future.

## Policies for Thais abroad

Our election discussion brought questions from the overseas Thai community to political-party representatives. The discussion outline focused on why people seek opportunities abroad, how parties would support Thai workers and entrepreneurs overseas, and what would make returning to Thailand a realistic choice.

One theme was particularly close to my experience as a researcher: how Thailand could create opportunities for people trained abroad to use their skills. The questions covered government scholarships, research infrastructure, suitable career paths, and the frustration of returning to a system that cannot fully use someone’s training. The outline also raised questions about political exiles, safety, and confidence in the justice system.

## The Wall of Senator — ฝ่ากำแพง ส.ว.

The post-election discussion turned to the Senate’s role in choosing a prime minister and the challenges of forming a government. With perspectives from civil-society participants, the discussion considered how elected representatives, senators, the media, and the public could influence what happened next.

Questions included the tension between compromise and maintaining political commitments, the debate around Section 112, and the role of Thais overseas. These were conversations grounded in the uncertainty of 2023, offering a record of the questions people were asking at the time.
