The 2026 Thai SynBio Challenge shifts the focus from colorful proteins to plasmid design. Called **The Arena — Survival of the Engineered**, it frames a biological design problem as a Battle Royale: how will an engineered plasmid perform when its rivals enter the same environment?

## The Arena

Teams design a plasmid within a 5 kb limit, with a unique DNA barcode for tracking. The designs are tested in a shared *E. coli* BL21(DE3) population, and barcode sequencing is used to assess their relative representation. The competition’s stated goal is to achieve the highest plasmid copy number in the mixed population.

## Design meets strategy

The competition includes an Intelligence and Design phase. Finalists can study the other teams’ strategy videos and revise their own designs before synthesis. This adds a strategic dimension: a design must be considered in relation to the other entries, as well as on its own merits.

The challenge begins in 2026, with the Grand Arena final scheduled for **May 2027 at Naresuan University**. This post introduces the challenge; results are still to come.
