I help moderate the SynBio4ALL webinar series with Sebunya Emmanuel Kato, bringing researchers, educators, and biotechnology practitioners into conversation with African scientific communities. The series connects research with questions about access, education, and opportunities to put new ideas into practice.

Our conversations have explored carbon capture with Michael Köpke, synthetic cells with Kate Adamala, bioprospecting and biobusiness with Max van 't Hof, and accessible biology with Sebastian Cocioba. Other sessions cover plant synthetic biology, the bioeconomy, and preparing for graduate school.

The recordings offer a way to revisit these discussions and discover perspectives from across the field.

[Explore the webinar archive](https://synbioforall.org/webinars/) or [watch the YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOWKJieX8rKZsxR2GcLYyQ164zipwLiQo).
