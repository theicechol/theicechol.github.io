I co-founded the biweekly podcast series "Get Rich with SynBio," or "รวยด้วย SynBio," with the Biology Beyond Nature team in Thailand. The series brought together Thai synthetic-biology researchers and science communicators from around the world to discuss developments and opportunities in synthetic biology and biotechnology. We produced 26 episodes over two years.
