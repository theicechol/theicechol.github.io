In 2024, I joined How To Grow (Almost) Anything (HTGAA) as a committed listener. The course brought together synthetic biology, hands-on design, and a global community of learners. I documented my participation in Notion and shared my experience with CRISPR design in an HTGAA Asia session.

## My course notes

[Explore my HTGAA 2024 Notion page](https://observant-clef-d1d.notion.site/Cholpisit-Ice-Kiattisewee-fe2dd3f72567457394f07f214ce5dd94) for my course documentation and project work.

My final-project work connected the course with my interest in antimicrobial resistance, exploring the growth costs associated with antibiotic-resistance genes. I planned and analyzed the project while collaborating with Austin and a student team at VISTEC in Thailand.

## Practical CRISPR Design — HTGAA Asia

I also gave a talk on **Practical CRISPR Design** for the Asia session, bringing my research experience into the learning community.
