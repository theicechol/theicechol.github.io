In 2026, I continued as a teaching assistant for How To Grow (Almost) Anything (HTGAA), leading several recitations and labs. My teaching connected DNA design and assembly with cell-free systems and CRISPR-based metabolic engineering.

## Selected recitations

- **DNA Gel Art, Restriction Enzymes, Benchling, and Twist:** connecting sequence design with experimental planning and DNA visualization.
- **Cell-Free Protein Synthesis:** a recitation with Ben Arias-Almeida on using cellular machinery outside living cells.
- **CRISPR-based Metabolic Engineering:** connecting gene regulation with the design of microbial production systems.
- **Golden Gate Assembly:** a session on modular DNA assembly and its use in building biological constructs.

[Find recitation recordings and slides in the 2026 weekly archive](https://pages.htgaa.org/2026a/course-pages/weeks/index.print.html).
